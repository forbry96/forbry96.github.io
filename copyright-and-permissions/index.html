Copyright & Permissions

I want people to use this course. It is free because I want Christians to be able to work through the material, check the sources, and use what they learn in real conversations. The copyright notice is not meant to get in the way of that.

What you are welcome to do

You do not need to ask permission to share a link to the website or an individual lesson. You are also welcome to use the course for your own study, teach through it in a church or class, or work through it with your family or small group.

If you want to refer to an argument in your own teaching or quote a short passage, that is fine. Please credit Learn Classical Apologetics and link back to the relevant lesson when possible.

When to ask first

Please contact me before copying substantial portions of the original course onto another website, distributing copies of entire lessons, making a derivative course from its original wording or materials, or selling reproductions of the content.

This is not meant to prevent ordinary quotation or uses that the law already allows. I just want the original work to remain available here rather than being copied and presented as somebody else's course.

What this does and does not cover

These permissions concern original material from this project. The arguments themselves, historical facts, and other ideas discussed here are not mine to own. Scripture quotations, outside articles, linked resources, and other third-party material may have their own copyright terms.

If you have another use in mind, or you are not sure whether you need permission, feel free to ask.
